# Example 1: Exam Preparation

## Scenario
A student needs to learn single-variable calculus for a final exam in 8 weeks. They have 10 hours per week to study, but their algebra is weak.

## Input

```
I need to learn calculus for my final exam in 8 weeks. I can study about 10
hours per week. I took algebra a while ago and I'm pretty rusty, so I think
I'll need to refresh that first. I learn best with video lectures and practice
problems.
```

## Expected Output Structure

```markdown
# Personalized Learning Path — Single-Variable Calculus for Final Exam

## 1. Learner Profile Summary

- **Goal**: Learn single-variable calculus to pass final exam
- **Current Level**: Weak algebra, needs prerequisite refresh
- **Time Budget**: 10 hours/week for 8 weeks (80 hours total)
- **Deadline**: 8 weeks from start
- **Modality**: Video lectures + practice problems

## 2. Learning Frameworks Applied

### Anchor Frameworks (Always Applied)

**Backward Design (Understanding by Design)**
- **Authors**: Wiggins & McTighe
- **Rationale**: Provides structured approach ensuring alignment with exam
- **Application**: All objectives map to exam tasks, assessments mimic exam format

**Bloom's Taxonomy (Revised)**
- **Authors**: Anderson & Krathwohl
- **Rationale**: Ensures objectives progress appropriately through cognitive levels
- **Application**: Early modules at Understand/Apply levels, later at Analyze

### Additional Frameworks (Selected for This Learner/Goal)

**Spaced Repetition & Retrieval Practice**
- **Researchers**: Bjork, Roediger
- **Rationale**: 8-week timeline requires optimized retention strategies
- **Application**: Comprehensive review schedule with practice exams

**Cognitive Load Theory**
- **Author**: Sweller
- **Rationale**: Calculus introduces complex concepts, manage cognitive load
- **Application**: Scaffolding for difficult topics like limits and derivatives

**Mastery Learning**
- **Author**: Bloom
- **Rationale**: Cumulative subject requiring mastery before advancing
- **Application**: Mastery checks before moving to dependent topics

## 3. Week-by-Week Learning Path

### Week 1: Algebra Prerequisite Refresh
**Learning Objective**: [Apply] Solve algebraic equations and manipulate expressions needed for calculus.

**Activities**:
- Algebra refresh videos (3 hours)
- Equation manipulation practice (4 hours)
- Function review exercises (3 hours)

**Resources**:
- Khan Academy: Algebra Basics
- PatrickJMT: Algebra Videos

**Formative Assessment**: Complete 20 algebra problems with 85% accuracy
**Hours**: 10/10
**Prerequisites**: None

### Week 2-3: Limits and Continuity
**Learning Objective**: [Understand] Explain concept of limits and evaluate basic limits.

**Activities**:
- Limit concept videos (4 hours)
- Limit calculation practice (6 hours)
- Continuity exploration (4 hours)

**Resources**:
- 3Blue1Brown: Essence of Calculus
- Professor Leonard: Calculus 1 Lectures

**Formative Assessment**: Solve 15 limit problems with 80% accuracy
**Hours**: 14 (10 + 4 buffer)

### Week 4-5: Derivatives and Differentiation Rules
**Learning Objective**: [Apply] Compute derivatives using basic rules and interpret meaning.

**Activities**:
- Derivative concept videos (4 hours)
- Differentiation rule practice (8 hours)
- Applied derivative problems (6 hours)

**Resources**:
- Khan Academy: Derivatives
- MIT OpenCourseWare: Single Variable Calculus

**Formative Assessment**: Compute 20 derivatives with 80% accuracy
**Hours**: 18

### Week 6-7: Applications of Derivatives
**Learning Objective**: [Analyze] Use derivatives to solve optimization and related rates problems.

**Activities**:
- Application concept videos (3 hours)
- Optimization problems (8 hours)
- Related rates practice (6 hours)
- Review of all topics (3 hours)

**Resources**:
- Professor Leonard: Applications of Derivatives
- Textbook: James Stewart Calculus

**Formative Assessment**: Solve 10 applied problems
**Hours**: 20

### Week 8: Exam Preparation and Review
**Learning Objective**: [Evaluate] Demonstrate comprehensive understanding through practice exams.

**Activities**:
- Practice exam 1 (3 hours)
- Review weak areas (4 hours)
- Practice exam 2 (3 hours)

**Resources**:
- Past exam papers
- Practice exam repository

**Summative Assessment**: Timed practice exam under exam conditions
**Hours**: 10

## 4. Assessment Structure

### Formative Assessments
- **Week 1**: Algebra refresher quiz (85% accuracy required)
- **Week 2-3**: Limit calculation check (80% accuracy)
- **Week 4-5**: Derivative computation quiz (80% accuracy)
- **Week 6-7**: Applied problems (individual feedback)
- **Week 8**: Practice exams under timed conditions

### Summative Milestones

#### Milestone 1: Prerequisite & Foundation Mastery — Week 3
- **Objectives Assessed**: Algebra refresh, Limits and Continuity
- **Assessment Task**: Comprehensive quiz covering algebra through limits
- **Mastery Criteria**: 80% overall, 75% on limits portion
- **Progression Gate**: Must achieve mastery to continue to derivatives

#### Milestone 2: Derivative Competence — Week 5
- **Objectives Assessed**: Derivative computation and basic applications
- **Assessment Task**: Derivative computation + basic application problems
- **Mastery Criteria**: 80% accuracy, clear understanding of derivative meaning
- **Progression Gate**: Required before advanced applications

#### Milestone 3: Final Exam Preparation — Week 8
- **Objectives Assessed**: All topics from algebra through applications
- **Assessment Task**: Full practice exam under timed conditions
- **Mastery Criteria**: 75% on practice exam (exam readiness threshold)
- **Progression Gate**: Final check before actual exam
```
