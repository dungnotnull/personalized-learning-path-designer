## Core Concepts & Frameworks
- **Bloom's Taxonomy (revised):** Remember→Understand→Apply→Analyze→Evaluate→Create; objectives at appropriate cognitive levels.
- **Backward Design / UbD (Wiggins & McTighe):** define objectives → evidence/assessments → learning activities.
- **Spaced repetition & retrieval practice (Bjork, Roediger):** distributed practice + testing effect for durable retention.
- **Deliberate practice (Ericsson):** focused, feedback-rich practice at the edge of ability.
- **Cognitive Load Theory (Sweller):** manage intrinsic/extraneous/germane load; scaffold complexity.
- **Zone of Proximal Development (Vygotsky):** target tasks just beyond current ability with support.
- **Mastery learning (Bloom):** advance on demonstrated mastery, not time.
